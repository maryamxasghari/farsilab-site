📲Add FarsiLab to your home screenOpens like an app, full screen, and works offline

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆️ (a square with an arrow). On newer iPhones, tap ••• first, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep Open as Web App on, then tap Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮ at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app, then Install.

Then unlock your games in the app

The app keeps its own unlocks, separate from the browser. Open FarsiLab from your home screen, tap 📷 Scan QR code at the top and scan the QR code in your PDF (or tap ⌨️ Type a code). Use the scan button inside the app, not the phone's camera app, which opens the browser instead.

Open each game once while you have internet. After that it also works offline, for example in the car or on a plane.
